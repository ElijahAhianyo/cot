Design discussions, project updates, and stories about building with Cot belong here. The guides remain the place to learn current behavior; a blog post can explore a decision in more depth or explain what changed at a particular time.

This is a preview of the blog, with one sample article. It contains no release announcements or claims of production experience.

## Design discussions

### [Why framework transition guides belong in the docs](../framework-transition-guides/)

A developer arriving from Django asks different first questions from someone arriving from Axum. The documentation can acknowledge that experience without maintaining six copies of every feature guide.

## Project updates and stories

Future posts can introduce releases, discuss design tradeoffs, or describe a real application's lessons. Release announcements should link to the versioned [release notes](../../releases/overview/). Case studies should identify the application, its constraints, and the evidence behind any performance claims.

## Looking for help moving frameworks?

Use [Coming from another framework](../../coming-from/overview/) for the maintained explanation guides covering Django, Laravel, Rails, Spring Boot, Axum, and Actix Web. Those pages are organized by your starting point rather than publication date.
